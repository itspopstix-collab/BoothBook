Privacy Policy — BoothBook

Last updated: November 2026

BoothBook provides point-of-sale (POS), inventory cost tracking, and booth event profit & loss management for small businesses and pop-up vendors.

1. Data We Process

  • Store configuration, product recipes, inventory lots, and POS order records created by you.
  • Account email address used for authentication.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On-Device Storage: Data is saved locally on your device for offline operation.
  • Supabase Cloud (Optional): Synced securely to your isolated account when Supabase Cloud mode is selected.
  • Google Sheets (Optional): When you connect Google Sheets, BoothBook uses the drive.file permission solely to create and update the BoothBook spreadsheet inside your own Google Drive. BoothBook cannot view or access any other files in your Google Drive.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your business or personal data with third parties. Google user data accessed via Google Workspace APIs is used strictly to store your own POS and booth records in your Google Sheet and is never used for advertising or AI model training.

4. Contact

For questions or data deletion requests, contact: itspopstix@gmail.com
